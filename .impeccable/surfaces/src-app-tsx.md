# Surface brief: Star Auction app (all screens)

Scope: the whole game app (home, setup, auction table, sold reveal, results, match, history, settings). Mode: Experience; the auction table is the work.
Audience: Arab football fans, two friends on one phone or laptop, plus solo games against the AI. Arabic is the default and English is mirrored.
Task: bid on the open lot, pass, or bluff. Read the bid, the turn and the budgets at arm's length.
Constraints: no club logos or player photos, en-US digits, AA contrast, reduced motion respected, zoom allowed, full keyboard play.
Memorable moment: every bid lands on the poster as a fresh ink pass, and SOLD is a slammed red stamp.
Unresolved: none.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every round is printed as tonight's fight bill. The lot is the headline act, set in full-measure wood type, and two corners, RED and BLUE, are billed against each other. This refuses the category default of glowing gold player cards on dark pitch-green.

OWN-WORLD: Poster-stock cream ground with paper grain. Wood-ink black, barn red (red corner), deep blue (blue corner), pressed gray, linen tan. Ink passes overprint with multiply blend and land a hair off-register. Wood-type bands fill the measure, separated by heavy black rules and star ornaments. Buttons are solid ink blocks (primary red), or blue outlined blocks with stars. Disabled is a dry ghost impression. There is a night-print variant: cream and red/blue ink on black stock.

STORY: The visitor sees whose corner is bidding, how much each corner has left, and what the open lot is worth. They raise, pass or bluff, and the bill restamps after every move until SOLD.

FIRST VIEWPORT (auction table, phone portrait 390×844):
- The top band is a ruled header with the round number, a row of 11 stars and the slot position.
- Below it, the bout band is full width: red corner name and budget, VS, blue corner name and budget.
- The open lot takes about 45% of the height: position kicker, player name in wood type fitted to the full measure, club and nation line, a stamped rating box and the value.
- The hidden lot sits beside it as a narrow folded "MYSTERY ★ TBA" strip.
- The current bid is giant numerals in the holder's ink with a corner label. The timer is a fuse rule that burns down.
- Bid blocks sit at the bottom, within thumb reach.
- On desktop the layout is three columns: red corner card | lot broadside | blue corner card.

FORM: Hatch Show Print letterpress wood-type poster (catalog challenger posters-covers-sleeves-hatch-show-print), chosen by the user over the assigned Stadium Scoreboard (position 6 of my list). Seed key e62dc813.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Signature interaction: the press-pull. Each new bid is stamped in as one press: a quick scale-down with an ink-spread settle, and the previous bid stays behind as a dry ghost impression. SOLD is a rotated rubber stamp slam. Reduced motion becomes an instant swap.
